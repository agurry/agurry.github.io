About Me

Army-tested leadership, business education, technical curiosity, and practical problem-solving.

I am a U.S. Army Master Sergeant and Brigade Quality Assurance Evaluator NCOIC with the 4th Brigade (MI), 102nd Training Division (MS), supporting the United States Army Reserve Military Intelligence Center. I enlisted in the United States Army in 2000 and have served in the Active Army and Army Reserve in leadership, training, inspection, transportation, maritime, and quality assurance roles.

My previous assignments include 80th Training Command (TASS) Inspector General NCO, Chief of Operations and Training for the Mission Command Technologies Integration Program, Vessel Master and Boatswain, Marine Operations NCO, Detachment Sergeant, Watercraft Operations NCO, Squad Leader, Coxswain, and Watercraft Operator.

As I transition toward military retirement, I am building a professional portfolio and consulting practice focused on process automation, workflow improvement, operations management, readiness reporting, logistics support, risk management, documentation systems, dashboards, training systems, knowledge management, and practical tools that reduce repetitive work and improve decision-making for employers, clients, and teams.

My focus: helping small businesses, military teams, offices, and organizations turn repeated frustration into clear, simple, measurable, useful systems.
