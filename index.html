About Me

Army-tested leadership, business education, technical curiosity, and practical problem-solving.

I am a U.S. Army Master Sergeant and Quality Assurance Evaluator with the 4th Brigade (MI), 102nd Training Division (MS). I enlisted in the United States Army in 2000 and have served in the Active Army and Army Reserve in leadership, training, inspection, transportation, maritime, and quality assurance roles.

My previous assignments include 80th Training Command (TASS) Inspector General NCO, Chief of Operations and Training for the Mission Command Technologies Integration Program, Vessel Master and Boatswain, Marine Operations NCO, Detachment Sergeant, Watercraft Operations NCO, Squad Leader, Coxswain, and Watercraft Operator.

As I transition toward military retirement, I am building a professional consulting practice focused on process automation, workflow improvement, documentation systems, dashboards, training systems, and practical tools that reduce repetitive work and improve decision-making.

My focus: helping small businesses, military teams, offices, and organizations turn repeated frustration into clear, simple, measurable, useful systems.
